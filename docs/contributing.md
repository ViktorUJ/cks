# Community and contributing

We are building this platform to make knowledge and practical experience available to the community. The project grows when people share materials, ask questions, test exercises, and help fix mistakes.

There are many ways to help:

- Add a course chapter, lab, practical scenario, or worked example.
- Fix a technical error, inaccuracy, typo, or broken link.
- Make an explanation clearer, expand an example, or improve an exercise check.
- Add or update a translation.
- Complete a lab and share your feedback.

## How to propose a change

1. Choose the material you want to add or improve. If you are planning a substantial new section, discuss it in [Issues](https://github.com/ViktorUJ/cks/issues) or reach us through the [contacts](#contacts).
2. Fork the repository and make your changes on a separate branch, following the structure of the existing materials.
3. For a technical correction, describe the problem and how to verify the fix. For a new lab, include launch instructions, tasks, result validation, and a solution, following the existing examples.
4. Open a [pull request](https://github.com/ViktorUJ/cks/pulls) with a brief description of your changes.

If you are not ready to prepare a fix, report the problem in [Issues](https://github.com/ViktorUJ/cks/issues). For lab issues, include the lab number, steps to reproduce, and the validation result.

New contributors are always welcome. If you would like to help develop the project, [contact us](#contacts). We follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How to support the project

- Star the [repository](https://github.com/ViktorUJ/cks) to help other people discover it.
- Share the platform with colleagues or your study group.
- Write about your experience on [dev.to](https://dev.to/), [Medium](https://medium.com/), [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com), or your personal blog.
